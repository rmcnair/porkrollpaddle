What?

An Amy and Ryan concoction to paddle around NJ. We'll start from Port Jervis on the top-left of NJ, go down the Delaware and up the coast (through the bays) to the Hudson near the top-right of NJ. At Bear Mountain State Park, we'll drop the boat and hike back to Port Jervis on the Appalachian Trail (about 500 miles paddling, 65 miles hiking). If you live anywhere near the route (i.e. near the border or coast of NJ) and are open to letting us (Amy and Ryan) camp in your yard or stay in your house for a night, reach out below!

When?

We're launching on August 9th, 2026 and need to be finished up by September 8th (ideally also 2026).

Why?

To explore our home state. To meet interesting people and see pretty places. To eat good italian food and cheesecake. To spend the month paddling. To avoid the Texas summer. To have an adventure. To have some fun.

The Ask

There's not much camping along the way. If you live near the route and are open to letting us crash there for the night, please reach out. We're happy to camp in your yard and promise to have some interesting stories :) If this is something you might be open to, fill out the old form below and I'll reach out.
